Reading Text: Modern Supermarkets

Most modern supermarkets are carefully designed to encourage customers to spend more money. For example, fresh fruit, vegetables, and baked goods are often placed near the entrance because their bright colors and pleasant smells create a welcoming atmosphere. Basic necessities like milk, eggs, and bread are usually located at the back of the store. This layout forces shoppers to walk past shelves full of snacks, drinks, and special offers before they reach the items they actually came to buy.

In recent years, technology has also transformed the supermarket experience. Many stores now offer self-checkout machines, which allow customers to scan and pay for their own groceries without waiting in long lines. Online grocery shopping and home delivery have also become extremely popular, especially for busy families. Despite these changes, supermarkets remain an essential part of daily life in almost every town and city around the world.
